# CamboAI University Program (Trading Science)

## Program Outcomes
- Master multi-asset technical analysis and execution.
- Design, test, and operate robust discretionary/systematic strategies.
- Manage risk professionally with quantitative controls and review processes.
- Communicate trade rationales and results with institutional rigor.

## Structure (2 x 12-week terms)
- Term 1 (Foundations): TA101, RM101, DATA101, PSY101, Labs 1–3, Assignments A1–A3
- Term 2 (Application): TA201, STR201, STR301, OPT101/OPT201, Labs 4–5, Assignments A4–A5 (Capstone)

## Credit & Grading
- Each course: 3 credits
- Grading breakdown (program-level baseline):
  - Quizzes: 20%
  - Labs: 25%
  - Assignments/Projects: 35%
  - Participation/Reviews: 20%

## Tracks
- Discretionary Trader (TA + execution focus)
- Systematic Trader (TA + backtesting/process focus)
- Options Specialist (directional + income structures)

## Capstone
- Deliver a fully documented strategy with backtest, forward test, risk plan, and live execution rules.
