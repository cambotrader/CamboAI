# Program Syllabus (24 Weeks)

## Term 1 (Weeks 1–12)
- Week 1: Market Microstructure, Sessions, Order Types
- Week 2: Charting Foundations (OHLC, timeframes); TA101 Quiz 1
- Week 3: Trend/Structure; MTF mapping; Lab 1 (VWAP)
- Week 4: Indicators I (MA/RSI/MACD/ATR); Assignment A1 (Trade Plan)
- Week 5: Risk Core (R, RR, ATR stops, heat); RM101 Quiz 1
- Week 6: Data & Backtesting I; Lab 2 (RSI/MACD setups)
- Week 7: Pattern Library; journaling framework; Assignment A2 (10-Trade Journal)
- Week 8: Execution, slippage, spreads, checklists
- Week 9: Psychology I; error taxonomy; PSY101 Quiz 1
- Week 10: Data & Backtesting II; Lab 3 (Mean Reversion backtest)
- Week 11: Review Week; Assignment A3 (Backtest Report)
- Week 12: Midterm Oral Defense

## Term 2 (Weeks 13–24)
- Week 13: TA Advanced (divergence, harmonics); TA201 Quiz 1
- Week 14: Strategies I (Trend/Mean Reversion); STR201 Quiz 1
- Week 15: Breakout Systems; Lab 4 (MTF alignment)
- Week 16: Scalping/VWAP; Assignment A4 (Options Strategy Report)
- Week 17: Options Basics (greeks); OPT101 Quiz 1
- Week 18: Options Advanced (skew/structures); Lab 5 (Greeks experiments)
- Week 19: FX/Futures nuances; risk per contract; STR301 Quiz 1
- Week 20: System health, monitoring, dashboards
- Week 21: Capstone planning; dataset selection
- Week 22: Capstone build; advisor review
- Week 23: Capstone forward test and journal
- Week 24: Capstone defense (Assignment A5)
