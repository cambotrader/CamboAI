# CamboAI Trading Education Curriculum

## Tracks
- Foundations: Market structure, order types, risk basics
- Technical Analysis: Price action, indicators, patterns
- Strategy: Trend, mean-reversion, breakouts, scalping
- Asset Classes: Stocks, Crypto, FX, Futures, Options
- Execution: Entries, exits, sizing, automation
- Psychology: Discipline, journaling, error reduction

## Modules
1. Market Microstructure 101
2. Chart Basics: OHLC, timeframes, sessions
3. Risk Management Core (R, RR, heat)
4. Candlestick Patterns and Context
5. Support/Resistance and Liquidity Zones
6. Trend, Momentum, Pullbacks, Breakouts
7. Indicators: MA, RSI, MACD, ATR, VWAP
8. Multi-timeframe Analysis
9. Pattern Recognition: Harmonics, triangles, flags
10. Scalping Playbook (1m–5m)
11. Swing Playbook (1h–1d)
12. Options for Direction and Hedging
13. Futures and FX Nuances
14. Crypto Specifics (24/7, funding, truncation)
15. Backtesting and Walk-forward
16. Trade Plan, Journaling, Review

## Outcomes
- Build a rule-based strategy with clear risk
- Execute consistently with data-driven feedback
- Adapt per asset class and volatility regime
