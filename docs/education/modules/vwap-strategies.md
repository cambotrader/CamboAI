# VWAP Strategies

## Concepts
- VWAP is the intraday mean price weighted by volume; key for institutions
- Reclaim/Reject of VWAP often marks bias shifts or continuation

## Tools
- Session VWAP, Anchored VWAP (events), VWAP bands (±1–2σ)

## Long Scenarios
- Opening drive, pullback to VWAP, hold and higher low; re-acceleration
- Midday reclaim of VWAP after morning selloff, with rising cumulative delta

## Short Scenarios
- Opening pop, fail at VWAP band + rejection w/ lower high; breakdown

## Risk/Management
- Stop just past VWAP/band; take partials at 1R; exit on VWAP loss/reclaim

## Tips
- Avoid chop: wait for structure; confirm with volume/delta
