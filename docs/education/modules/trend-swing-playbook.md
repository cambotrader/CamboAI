# Trend and Swing Playbook

## Objective
Trade established trends with pullback entries and defined invalidation, or swing reversals from key levels.

## Timeframes
- Trend: 15m/1h with 4h/1d anchor
- Swing: 1h/4h with 1d anchor

## Indicators
- 20/50/200 EMA, RSI(14), MACD(12/26/9), ATR(14)

## Trend Entries
- Higher high + higher low structure; pullback to EMA20/50; RSI holds 40–60; MACD histogram turns up
- Breakout continuation from range with retest and hold above level

## Swing Reversal Entries
- At HTF level (S/R, VWAP bands), bullish/bearish divergence on RSI/MACD, reclaim of broken MA

## Stops and Targets
- Stop beyond last swing/ATR(1–1.5x)
- First target at +1.5R to 2R; trail under HL/LH or MA20/50

## Checklist
- HTF bias aligned
- Liquidity and spreads acceptable
- No imminent major news/earnings
