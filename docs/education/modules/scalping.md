# Scalping Playbook

## Objective
Capture small, repeatable edges on low timeframes (1m–5m), focusing on liquidity and momentum bursts.

## Setup
- Timeframes: 1m/5m with 15m anchor
- Indicators: VWAP, 20/50 EMA, RSI(14), ATR(14)
- Markets: High-liquidity stocks, FX majors, liquid crypto pairs

## Entry Triggers
- EMA20 > EMA50, price pullback to EMA20, RSI 40–60, impulse above micro swing
- VWAP reclaim with increasing volume and higher lows
- Breakout of tight range after volatility contraction

## Risk
- Initial stop: beyond last swing/ATR(1x)
- Position size: risk 0.25–0.5R per scalp, max daily risk 2R

## Management
- Partial at +1R, move stop to BE after structure confirms
- Exit on VWAP rejection or momentum fade (MACD hist crosses zero)

## Playbook Checklist
- Liquidity adequate (tight spreads)
- Trend alignment across 1m/5m/15m
- No major news in next 15 minutes
- Volatility within normal ATR percentiles
