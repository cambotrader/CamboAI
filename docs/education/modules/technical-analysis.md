# Technical Analysis Essentials

## Price Action
- **Structure**: HH/HL (uptrends), LH/LL (downtrends)
- **Context**: Where is price relative to key levels and session VWAP?

## Key Indicators
- **Moving Averages**: 20/50/200 EMA/SMA for trend and pullbacks
- **RSI (14)**: Momentum and mean reversion zones
- **MACD (12/26/9)**: Momentum shifts, crosses, histogram
- **ATR**: Volatility; use for stops and position sizing
- **VWAP**: Intraday mean; reclaim/reject signals

## Patterns
- **Continuation**: Flags, pennants, triangles
- **Reversal**: Double tops/bottoms, head & shoulders, harmonic patterns

## Multi-timeframe
- Map HTF bias (1h/4h/1d), align LTF entries (5m/15m)

## Risk Integration
- Stops at invalidation, position size by ATR and account risk
