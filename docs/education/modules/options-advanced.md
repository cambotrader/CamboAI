# Options Advanced

## Volatility and Skew
- IV rank/percentile: contextualize premium
- Skew: vertical (OTM puts richer in equities), calendar (term structure)

## Structures
- Vertical spreads: debit (directional), credit (income, mean reversion)
- Calendars/Diagonals: theta harvesting + directional bias
- Iron Condors: range-bound, manage wings by IV and risk appetite
- Ratio spreads & risk reversals (advanced)

## Strategy Selection Matrix
- Trend/momentum + low IV: debit spreads
- Range-bound + high IV: credit spreads/iron condors
- Event-driven: calendars/diagonals (earnings caution)

## Risk & Adjustments
- Position sizing by max loss; portfolio Greeks awareness
- Adjust losers early: roll, convert to butterflies/condors, or exit
- Hedge tails with cheap OTM options in high-vol regimes

## Execution
- Mid-price negotiation; avoid illiquid chains
- Scale-in: ladder entries across strikes/expiries

## Tracking
- Greeks exposure (Delta/Gamma/Theta/Vega), IV changes impact, P&L attribution by greeks
