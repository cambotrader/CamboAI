# Risk Management Core

## Principles
- Define max risk per trade (e.g., 0.5–1.0%)
- Use ATR- or structure-based stops
- Aim for RR >= 1.5:1 on average

## Position Sizing
- Size = (Account * Risk%) / StopDistance

## Heat & Limits
- Daily loss cap (e.g., 2–3R), stop trading if hit
- Limit concurrent correlated exposure

## Process
- Pre-plan entries/exits
- Journal trades and review weekly metrics
