# Options Basics

## Core Terms
- Calls, Puts, Strike, Expiry, ITM/ATM/OTM, Premium, IV, Greeks (Delta, Gamma, Theta, Vega)

## Simple Strategies
- Directional: Buy calls/puts with defined risk
- Income/Range: Credit spreads, Iron condors
- Trend: Debit spreads for RR improvement

## Practical Notes
- Avoid buying high IV near earnings unless explicitly playing volatility
- Position sizing with max premium at risk; plan exits
- Use spreads to manage theta/vega
