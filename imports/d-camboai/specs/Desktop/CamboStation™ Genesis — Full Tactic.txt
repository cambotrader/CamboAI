🎓 Advanced Education Center: Curriculum spans trading stocks, crypto, options, forex, commodities, bonds, warrants, T-Bills. Includes long-term investing, swing setups, retirement planning, and macro education. Supports image upload explainer, mentorship mode, quizzes, flashcards, and AI tutor. Built with Jupyter Binder, GitBook, Streamlit, MkDocs.

📔 AI Trading Journal & Journey Analyzer: Logs every trade with ticker, setup logic, emotions, screenshots, R:R tagging, sentiment state, and outcome. AI detects behavioral trends (FOMO, overconfidence, early exits), suggests corrections, and builds psychological profile. Entries linked to strategy planner and trade replay engine.
